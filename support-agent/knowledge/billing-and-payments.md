# Billing and payments

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices are generated on the first business day of each month and emailed to the
billing contact on the account. Customers can download every invoice from
**Account > Billing > Invoices**. Receipts are issued automatically after a
successful card payment.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
A pending authorisation can appear twice for up to five business days and then
disappears automatically. If a duplicate charge has settled, a billing
specialist must confirm it before any refund is promised. Ask the customer for
the invoice number and the last four digits of the card, never the full card number.

## Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of renewal. Monthly plans
are not refunded for partial months, but a billing specialist can issue account
credit as a goodwill gesture. Refunds return to the original payment method in
5-10 business days.

## Changing a payment method
Account owners can update the card or bank details under
**Account > Billing > Payment methods**. Failed payments are retried three times
over seven days before the subscription is paused.
