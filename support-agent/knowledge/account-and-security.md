# Account and security

## Password reset
Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page with **Forgot password**.
The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, ask them to
check the spam folder and confirm the address on the account.

## Locked accounts
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after five failed sign-in attempts. An account
specialist can unlock an account early only after verifying the customer's
identity through the registered email address.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication can be enabled under **Settings > Security**. If a
customer lost access to their authenticator, an account specialist must complete
an identity check before MFA is reset. Never disable MFA based on an email request alone.

## Closing an account or exporting data
Account owners can export their data from **Settings > Privacy > Export**.
Closing an account is permanent after a 14-day grace period.
