# General information

## Support hours and response times
Human specialists work Monday to Friday, 09:00-18:00 (Myanmar Time, UTC+06:30).
Every request receives an AI-assisted draft immediately, and a specialist reviews
and approves the reply before it is sent, normally within one business day.

## Plans and pricing
The Starter plan is free for up to three users. The Team plan adds shared
workspaces and priority support. Enterprise plans include single sign-on and a
named account manager. Exact prices are listed on the public pricing page.

## Contacting a human
Every reply in this support portal is approved by a human specialist. Customers
can follow up by submitting a new request that references their ticket ID.

## Privacy
We never ask for full card numbers, passwords or API keys in a support request.
